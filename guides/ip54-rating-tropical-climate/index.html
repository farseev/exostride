IP54 explained: what tropical-grade sealing means for an exoskeleton

Smoother Commutes Specs, explained Updated 2026-08-19
The short answer

What does an IP54 rating mean, and is it rain-proof?

IP54 means two things. The 5 is dust: dust cannot enter in a quantity that interferes with operation. The 4 is water: protected against splashing water from any direction. In practice that covers a Singapore afternoon downpour, a humid platform, and trail dust — but not submersion, not a pressure hose, and not being left in a puddle. For a device you are meant to wear every day, IP54 is the difference between one that goes out in the rain and one that stays home.

Reading the two digits

IP stands for Ingress Protection, and the digits are independent:

  • First digit (5) — solids. Dust-protected: some ingress is allowed, but not enough to affect operation.
  • Second digit (4) — liquids. Protected against water splashed from any direction, tested from every angle.
  • What it does not certify — immersion (that starts at 7), pressure jets (6), or long-term standing water.

Why this rating matters more here

In a temperate climate an unsealed device is inconvenient. In Singapore it is unusable. Rain arrives without much notice, most walking routes include an unsheltered stretch, and ambient humidity sits high enough year-round that condensation is a real consideration for any powered wearable. Sealing is not a premium feature here; it is the entry requirement.

Living with it sensibly

Walk in the rain; do not wade. Wipe it down and let it air-dry rather than sealing it damp into a bag. Trail dust and sweat salt are handled by the same rating, but a rinse after a coastal or beach walk is still sensible. None of this adds weight — the unit is still 2.4 kg with the sealing in place.

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear it in a Singapore downpour?
Yes. IP54 covers splashing water from any direction, which includes heavy rain while walking. Avoid full submersion and do not wade through standing water above the unit.
Can I rinse it under a tap?
A light rinse and a wipe is fine. A pressure jet is not — that requires IPx6, which IP54 does not claim.
Did the sealing make it heavier or bulkier?
No. It remains 2.4 kg and foldable.
